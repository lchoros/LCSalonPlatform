## Decisions made
- **Next was a receipt**, because nothing in the app gave the client a record of what they paid. Every payment was
  already recorded, but the desk had nothing to hand over or send.
- **Tier 2**: it shows money to a client and sends a client an email. It is labelled `needs-owner`.
- **Read from the bill, never stored**, so the receipt says what the till says, including a method put right later
  and a product brought back. A test holds that the payments on a receipt add up to its total.
- **Sent only on a tap**, to the address on the card, and only when the salon sends emails to clients.
- **No print button**: the app has no script of its own, and the browser's Print does it. The page hides the menus
  when printed.
- **Left out on purpose**: fees from earlier visits paid on the same bill, receipts for counter sales and vouchers,
  sending it by itself, VAT lines. All named in the ADR.

## What was learned
- Proving a guard with `if (false)` proves nothing here: warnings are errors, so the build fails and the tests run
  against the last good build and pass. Take the guard's effect out with code that compiles (the `return` replaced),
  and check that the run built.
- A method name in the middle of a sentence ("Paid earlier by Card at the salon's terminal") reads badly; "Paid
  earlier: Card at the salon's terminal" is the checkout's own style.
- A booking test checks that an inactive service named "Hidden" is not on the page, ignoring case, so the Tailwind
  class `print:hidden` on the layout failed it. The layout now uses a class of our own, `screen-only`, left out
  when printed; the test is unchanged.
