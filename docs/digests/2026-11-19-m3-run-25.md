## Decisions made
- **Team logins straight after sign-up**: the owner's login existed, the receptionist's could not. The user
  service had every mutation already; the page was the missing piece.
- **The salon always keeps one active owner login**: the last one can be neither demoted nor turned off — in the
  form and in the handler both, proven by guard removal.
- **A starting password handed over in person**, not an email invitation: phase 1 has no real email, and the
  stamp rotation (ADR 0009) already signs a person out everywhere when it changes.
- **Off, never deleted**: payment history references users; a turned-off login fails sign-in exactly like a wrong
  password.
