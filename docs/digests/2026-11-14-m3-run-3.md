## What the owner asked for and what was done
- **"Add all services from Studio 20."** The demo catalogue is the salon's price list: 47 services in five
  categories, 66 rows with variants. Keratin has its six named variants.
- **"This has variants as well."** A variant is a service with a shared title and a label; clients see one entry
  with a price range.
- **"Filters, searching, pagination, as reusable components."** `_SearchBox`, `_FilterTiles`, `_Pager`, `Paged<T>`
  and `ServiceCatalogue`, used by the booking page and the admin services list.
- **"More squared, too much height on calendar days."** Day tiles are 56 px squares.
- **"Keep the UI more like that."** White cards with a thin two-colour border and a soft glow, squared tiles, on the
  booking flow. The admin pages are not restyled yet.

## Filled in by guess, for the owner to correct
- **Durations.** The price list has none; every duration is an estimate.
- **Variants of 14 services.** Only Keratin's are known. The others have two options, "Option 1" and "Option 2", at
  the lowest and highest price of their range.
- **Phone and email.** Not in the demo data, because the repository is public. The street address is.

## Not done, and why
- **Stripe sandbox.** Tier 2 and a new runtime dependency. It needs two decisions from the owner first: one Stripe
  account for the platform or Stripe Connect per salon, and what a client pays online (the deposit, or the whole
  price). The secret key pasted in chat is not stored anywhere by the agent; it should be rolled.
- **Restyle of the admin pages.** Its own PR, so this one stays reviewable.

## What was learned
- Replacing demo data touches every test that named it. Tests of seeding mechanics should name as little data as
  they can.
- A perl replacement ate the backslash of a `·` escape again; the updated seeder test caught it at once.
