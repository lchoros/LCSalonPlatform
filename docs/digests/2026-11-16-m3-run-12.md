## The decision: what makes it different
The competition is a marketplace that owns the client and makes leaving hard. This product is the opposite, and the
features chosen for this run are the ones that make that visible to a salon owner in the first week:

1. **The salon can take everything away, in one place.** No other system offers its client list for the asking.
2. **The salon sees what it really keeps**, not only what it took: profit on what it sells.
3. **The desk sells a bottle to somebody who walks in**, without making up a visit.
4. **The salon fills its own empty chairs** from its own waiting clients, with no marketplace in between.

Gift vouchers and consultation forms were left out on purpose. A voucher is money the salon owes, and a
consultation form holds health data; each needs its own decision by the owner.

## Decisions made
- **The client list is for the owner only** (ADR 0051). A manager works with clients one by one; taking the whole
  list is the owner's.
- **Health notes are in no file.** The slice does not read them at all.
- **Each download of the client list is logged** with who and how many rows. A page that shows that record is not
  built.
- **The client import drops the apostrophe a spreadsheet file puts before a phone number.** Without it the salon's
  own file could not be read back.
- **The cost of the day is kept on the bill line when it is paid** (ADR 0052), so the profit of a past sale does not
  move when a supplier changes a price. A unit without a cost is not guessed at; the report counts such units and,
  with no cost at all, states no profit instead of zero. Sales made before this change have no cost on record.
- **A counter sale is a line of money with no client** (ADR 0053). Only that kind may lack a client, and a check
  constraint in the database enforces it. Its products are rows of their own table; the visit's bill is untouched.
- **The counter sale migration cannot be rolled back once a counter sale exists.** `Down` fails on purpose instead
  of inventing a client for money that has none.
- **Refunding a counter sale is not built.** It is the first thing to add after this.
- **The public "tell me when a time comes up" form was not built.** The text message check carries a booking and
  has to be generalised first; an unchecked public form would let anybody put anybody's number on the list. It
  wants its own run.

## What was learned
- A phone number that starts with a plus is a formula to a spreadsheet. The guard that makes a file safe has to be
  undone by the import that reads it back, and only for that case.
- A new table that points at ledger lines breaks every clean-up that deletes ledger lines. Here that was only the
  browser tests' fixture, and 35 tests failed at once in under half a minute: many failures that fast are one cause.
