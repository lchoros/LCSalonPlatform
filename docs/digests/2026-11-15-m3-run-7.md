## What the owner said and what was done
- **"PR done please continue."** Products and stock was the recommended next piece, so it was built.

## What was built
- A list of products with what is on each shelf, a "low" mark, search, a filter and pages.
- A form to add or change a product; a new one can start with today's count.
- A product page with four ways to change the shelf (a delivery, used in the salon, a count, a write-off) and the
  history of every change with who and when.
- Seven demo products for the demo salon, generic names, two of them low.

## Decisions made
- **The shelf only, not the sale.** Selling at the checkout takes money and is Tier 2; it comes as its own PR.
- **Whole units, never below zero, every change a line** (ADR 0036).
- **No new permission.** Everybody who works the diary records deliveries and uses; owner and manager add
  products, count and write off.

## Blocked
Nothing.

## What was learned
- A form that posts and returns to the same address needs a browser test that waits for the new text, not for the
  address. The product test does.
