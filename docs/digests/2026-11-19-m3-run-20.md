  are the periods that show growth and quiet seasons.
- **By week, Monday to Sunday, cut at the period's ends**, so every day is in exactly one bar; a year is at most 53.
- **One existing test changed with the behaviour**: it said 63 days have no chart; now it says 63 days are ten
  weekly bars.
