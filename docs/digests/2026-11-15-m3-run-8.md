## Decisions made (ADR 0037, the owner decides)
- **Stock leaves the shelf when the bill is paid**, not when the product is rung up.
- **A sale is never refused for a shelf that reads too low**: the record is counted up by what it was short, then
  the sale comes off. The history says "Found at a sale".
- **Products are their own line of money** (`ProductSale`), so the till and the reports tell retail from services.
- **An unpaid line can be taken off; a paid line never.** No refunds yet.
