## Tier 2 items for the owner to veto or revert
- **#39 CI workflow edit** (caches, CodeQL on `main` pushes only, `upload: never`, findings fail the job).
- **ADR 0011 health data** (merged in #42): allergies, patch-test and medical notes encrypted with ASP.NET Data
  Protection in one column, decrypted only on the client card; consent recorded with source, time and text version;
  GDPR/RODO export and erasure deferred to M3 with a note in the ADR.
- **ADR 0014 public booking identity** (#47): a client is identified by phone number and a 6-digit SMS code (5-minute
  life, 5 attempts, `LogSmsProvider` only in phase 1); a signed cancel token in the confirmation SMS; verification
  rows kept for a short retention (ADR 0017 sets the job).
- **Permission design for calendars** (ADR 0016, #50): the calendar needs `Bookings.Manage` **and** `Clients.View`
  because it shows client names; a Receptionist without `Clients.View` is denied the whole screen rather than shown an
  anonymised one. Easy to flip later.

## Decisions made (owner may veto) — ADRs 0010–0016
- 0010 staff + weekly working hours as the rota, no exceptions table at first; 0015 adds time off as busy intervals
  (no "closed" status; existing appointments inside new time off stay booked and are shown to the manager).
- 0012 one `appointments` table with a GiST exclusion constraint per staff member as the final guard against double
  booking, a pure `AvailabilityCalculator` with FsCheck properties, state machine `Booked → Confirmed → Arrived →
  Completed | NoShow | Cancelled` (Booked → Arrived allowed for walk-ins).
- 0013 categories as a flat ordered list, buffer and processing time on the service (processing time frees the staff
  member for another short visit).
- 0014 public booking is server-rendered, three taps to a slot on a phone, "Any staff" picks the least-booked person;
  `IPublicUrlBuilder` builds salon-host URLs for SMS links.
- 0016 the admin calendar is a CSS-grid timetable with links and small forms, no JavaScript; FullCalendar
  drag-and-drop deferred until the owner vendors the library (phase 1 forbids downloads in builders).
