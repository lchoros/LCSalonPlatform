## Tier 2 decisions for the owner to veto or revert

  Identity's EF store (no new package, tenant-owned users under RLS). Lockout 5 × 15 min, generic failure text with a
  decoy hash, security-stamp re-validation every 5 minutes, host-only cookies, cross-salon cookies dropped by the
  tenant middleware. Open question for the owner: on the bare platform domain an authenticated claim still resolves
  the tenant (kept from ADR 0002; a host-only cookie cannot reach it from a browser).

  architecture test and tenant-scoped consumers.
- Demo logins in Development only: `owner@studio-20.local` / `desk@studio-20.local`, password `Demo:OwnerPassword`
  from `appsettings.Development.json`.
